Empty the room takes the ambient crowd away, which is what EXPOSED means: the decoys are running but there is nothing to blend into.

Exposure check is the one to try if you only try one. It listens for a few seconds, spots the device that suddenly starts shouting, and lists the network names it is announcing it remembers. On real hardware that device is your phone and those names are yours. The four shown here are invented. They are written in the router-default style the project's own SSID pool uses, and none came from a capture.
