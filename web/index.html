SIMULACRA

Hide among shades. Watch for hunters.

Simulacra is a small multi-node fleet that buries your real devices in a crowd of phantom shades — synthetic BLE and Wi-Fi devices that look like an ordinary crowd — and flags hunters: trackers and followers that shadow you across time and place. It runs on cheap ESP32 boards and shows what it sees on a small display.

Raise a fleet

You need at least one decoy board (ESP32-C5 or ESP32-C6) and one controller (ESP32-2432S028, the "CYD" display). Plug in each board and flash it — the chip is detected and the right role is installed automatically.

Before you begin. Works in desktop Chrome or Edge only (Web Serial). This installs the baked starter build — every fleet shares one public key, so it is for trying Simulacra out, not a private deployment; build the provisioned regime from source for real use. A plain (non-CYD) ESP32 board would receive controller firmware, so use the boards above.
Simulacra is counter-surveillance by obfuscation. Source, docs, and the provisioned build: github.com/Em3ritus/simulacra.
